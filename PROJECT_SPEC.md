# Jules Berne’s Pie Chart — Project Specification

## Mandatory development protocol

**This is the authoritative description of the current intended app, not a release diary.** At the start of **every** development request, the assistant must read the current `PROJECT_SPEC.md`, consider it alongside the user's request, and inspect the relevant existing code. Preserve established behaviour, reuse shared components, and identify contradictions or design choices requiring the user's agreement **before** making unrequested design changes. The user does not maintain or need to refer to this file.

Every delivered development patch must also update this file **in place**: replace superseded requirements, remove obsolete text and duplication, distinguish implemented from unverified behaviour, and maintain an internally consistent current specification. Do not append change logs or speculative features. Where the code, specification, and observed behaviour disagree, investigate and flag the discrepancy; never silently redefine the requirement. Only changed files belong in each patch ZIP.

**Baseline:** PieChart web app v0.1.077; separate Android step companion v0.2.000. **Status:** Agreed product requirements below, with unresolved verification and work in the final section.

## 1. Purpose, architecture, and navigation

PieChart is a free, phone-first, practical nutrition, activity, water, and weight tracker, replacing Noom's recording functions but not its coaching. It is a lightweight GitHub Pages PWA (vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript and manifest), with Google sign-in/Firebase for data and a separate native Android Health Connect bridge for steps. Keep everyday entry quick, the approved visual design consistent, and unnecessary controls out of the interface. During development the PWA uses **standalone**, not automatic fullscreen, display; do not lock orientation.

The **graph is the home screen**. Its compact header uses the approved right-facing pie-slice-on-nautical-chart artwork, the app name, a stroked binoculars history toggle immediately left of the settings cog, and the existing header height. Settings links, in order, to **Food library**, **Activity library**, **Details & Targets**, and **Account**. Account holds Google sign-in/out, connection status, manual food-library JSON copy/export, and source attribution. No demo CSV upload/reset controls appear in Settings; developers edit `dummy-data.csv` outside the app. Old local CSV overrides are ignored.

Double-tapping a home metric opens its appropriate daily dialog: **Weight → Add Weight; Steps → Today's Activities; Water → Today's Water; Calories/Fibre/Protein/Fats/Carbs → Today's Food**. A double-tap must not accidentally activate a control in the dialog it opens. Each daily dialog uses the same styling and a stable **90% of visible viewport** height, with one internal scrolling area, keyboard-aware resizing, accessible Close, and no jumping when results change. Editing an individual FDM or activity hides the daily date arrows until returning to the daily root.

## 2. Graph, readouts, and history

The eight default vertically stacked bands, in order, are **Weight, Steps, Water, Calories, Fibre, Protein, Fats, Carbs**. Each retains its approved colour, independent native-unit scale, fine per-day fades, and full-width separators. On the right, display the current/viewed value, applicable target or calorie range, and percentage if a valid target exists; a 12 px edge strip has a muted background and proportional coloured fill. Fats and Carbs have **no invented targets** and display whole grams without a space before `g` (e.g., `18g`) in their large readouts; preserve greater underlying precision. Weight percentage means **actual ÷ target**, not progress towards a loss. The Fats icon must look fatty/oily and differ from Water. Hide/show bands only via explicit eye controls; reorder them by long-pressing and dragging right-hand labels, without changing visibility on a plain tap.

The graph uses the same upright left-to-right layout in portrait and landscape. Initially show **100 calendar days**; horizontal gestures adjust the visible historical range, moving the time axis during the gesture and redrawing on release. Use monthly date marks, switching to years for long periods. All bands share one exact daily x-position per calendar date, even for missing/partial records; never compress dates or aggregate daily values into multi-day averages. The plotted range **ends yesterday**: today's incomplete values appear in the right-hand readings only and cannot influence any plotted scale or weight envelope. Show the true last date on the axis. A calorie column represents **kilocalories consumed**, not a percentage of budget; its percentage readout is relative to that day's calculated budget and therefore need not follow bar height.

Each metric independently switches from the public sample CSV to real member data at **that metric's first real record** (weight, steps, water, and food-derived nutrition have separate cutoffs); activity markers do not shift unrelated cutoffs. Real records always win. The sample history is public, shared by both accounts, and never becomes private account history. Signed-out mode shows a clearly labelled sample chart. Absence of an actual value remains **unknown**, never fabricated zero. The public CSV has 114 genuine historical weigh-ins from **13 February–16 November 2026**; its other measures are illustrative, and its weights are not automatically imported into private records. Sample dates/weights may be used only for pre-personal historical budget calculations, never for today's weight or a personal period.

Plot actual weigh-in dots without white outlines. A smooth filled weight high/low envelope uses genuine measurements from a **seven-calendar-day window**, including prior off-screen context; never invent missing weigh-ins. Draw the Weight target line only if it is already within the natural band scale; never stretch the scale to reach it. Draw applicable target lines and calorie-budget low/high lines with the existing coloured dashed treatment; **the bounds pass through the exact calculated value at each day centre**, without smoothing values across days. Interpolation between centres is visual only. In signed-in sample history, use the member's current Weight, Water, Steps, Protein, and Fibre goals, never fictional CSV goals.

**History mode:** The binoculars toggle enters chart-date selection from today, then returns directly to today when tapped again. While selecting, a finger tap/drag previews a vertical date line and compact date popup; releasing chooses that date. The selected marker remains on the graph. Outside selection mode, chart taps do nothing, and range-zoom gestures work normally. The date popup has one-day back/forward arrows; there is no separate Past Date/Today button strip. Viewing a previous date gives the entire app/dialog a subdued lavender tone; advancing to today restores the normal tone. The plotted graph stays anchored to yesterday, rather than ending at the selected day. A selected date changes the readouts, diary lists, and destination of new entries, with no future weight leaking into earlier readings.

**Daily dialog navigation:** Today's Food, Water, and Activities each have **back/forward day arrows immediately left of Close**. They use the same shared selected-date state and history styling as the binoculars, moving exactly one calendar day, never past today or before available chart history. Their contents and recording dates update immediately. Returning to today restores live mode. The currently selected day is consistently respected by any nested editor, recording, or correction.

Exercise is represented on completed dates by **one small solid coral circle with a light outline near the foot of the Steps band**, regardless of how many activities occurred; it must not obscure the steps or target. A compact coral activity-count badge also appears by the Steps label for the viewed date, including today. No activity means no marker or badge; do not introduce a second step series or duration bars.

## 3. Daily recording and shared form behaviour

**Weight:** An ordinary numeric kg input replaces the old ruler. Prefill the last **genuine** weigh-in on or before the viewed date (blank if none), never sample weight. Allow typing to 0.01 kg, show two decimals, and scrub in 0.05 kg increments. Save separate owner-private, timestamped weigh-in events with the local calendar date; the last genuine weigh-in of a day is the chart value. A historical date with a real weigh-in offers **Delete recorded weight** with confirmation, deleting only the most recent event on that date; an earlier event on that date can then be shown. Preserve irregular records and support corrections. Today's weight is shown in the home readout, not today's plot.

**Today's Food:** Show a centred large-metric-style **Calories remaining of maximum budget** above the existing shared Food Library search and scanner. Display recorded FDM servings for the viewed day with name, quantity/unit, and an unobtrusive **Delete** control on the right (confirmation); **no plus/minus controls**. Tap an entry to edit its own quantity and date, scaling the original recorded nutrition/water snapshot rather than pulling in changed library nutrients. Keep a clearly separated **Favourites** list below the recorded entries. On search focus, hide both lists and show nothing until typing begins; then show the shared typeahead results. Clearing the query and leaving search restores the daily lists. The Favourites heading has greater spacing above than the preceding list, uses **14 px** item-sized type, and has the same tight divider gap as Recorded today. New food servings record to the viewed day and return to the Food Library search after recording. No compulsory meal-time categories.

**Today's Water:** Same daily-dialog styling and size. Show **litres remaining to today's water goal** (or unavailable/over-goal wording), then the selected day's recorded water-contributing food servings and legacy water entries in diary-row formatting. Below these show the smaller **Record Water** heading, five recent quick quantities, and a **New Amount** input. Do not automatically focus that field or open the keyboard; it supports the common hold-to-scrub gesture. Quick drinks are recorded as a Water **food-entry snapshot** using Water's configured contribution; old standalone `waterEntries` remain readable and counted, but are no longer written. Shortcut amounts stay private to the member. The shared Food Item named **Water** is required for shortcuts.

**Today's Activities:** Same daily-dialog styling and size. Show **steps remaining to the viewed day's step goal**, its recorded activities, and access to the shared Activity Library's search/favourites and recording flow. Do not use the old duration-and-effort-only form.

For all three readouts, calculate the remaining amount from the viewed day's real entries and available goal/budget, say **over** rather than showing a negative remainder, and say unavailable when there is no valid target. Historical dates use their own real records.

**Shared numeric and text inputs:** Use existing reusable field/popup components. Focusing a populated input selects its contents for replacement. Numeric touch scrubbing uses **one velocity-sensitive curve across fields**, activated only by a stationary **250 ms press-and-hold** with a subtle active indicator. A short tap opens typing; ordinary swiping scrolls instead of changing values, and holding must not trigger keyboard focus. Slow movement allows unit-appropriate precision (including **1 g** and **0.05 kg**); faster movement accelerates conservatively. A normal release stops; only a clearly fast flick coasts briefly with bounded, decaying momentum. Slowing, reversing, or a new touch restores fine control/stops inertia. Unit and precision determine the scale, not separate per-field gesture algorithms. Modal forms remain usable when the phone keyboard is open.

## 4. Food Library, FDMs, and recipes

**FDM** means Food Item, Dish, or Meal. Both accounts share the *definitions*, but each member has **private, cross-device favourites** and their own recording history. Approved library items are individually reviewed and intentionally saved; do not bulk-seed from references, temporary local snapshots, or generic sample foods. Old temporary approved-food test data are retired; preserve all genuine live shared Food Items and recipes. Names are **Capital Case** in storage and display. Do not label items “approved” in the interface.

Use **one Food Library search/scan/typeahead controller** wherever FDMs are chosen, including diary search and ingredient selection. Before searching, show only relevant personal favourites; do not automatically suggest recent but non-favourite FDMs or Activities. When typing, show matching items in this order: **Favourites → other matching saved FDMs → Add new Food Item → Make a Dish → Put together a Meal → NZ reference → Australian reference → USA/USDA reference**. Creation actions are ordinary result rows only while searching, not separate buttons. Within each group, rank by search relevance, then name, without recent-use ranking. Search may use food names or barcode numbers. Keep tags (Food Item, Dish, Meal) visible, with distinct green/orange/purple badges; source-only results look lighter and indicate provenance. No extra provider panels/headings or explanation cards. Keep input text at least 16 px without an added external focus outline; results scroll without visible scrollbar/gutter. Place the scanner preview at the top of the library card. After saving, clear/refocus search, except that saving an ingredient returns to the unfinished recipe with all unsaved details preserved.

Bundle locally searchable **read-only** reference indexes outside the shared library: **NZ Concise Food Composition Tables 14th edition (2021), 1,278 records; Australian Food Composition Database Release 3, 1,588; USDA Foundation Foods October 2026, 424** (3,290 total), in that priority order. Retain attribution, date, licence notices, and original references; incomplete source nutrients remain missing, not zero. The NZ live database has no verified public search API. For barcodes use **Open Food Facts only** at present; its nutrition is not guaranteed reliable. Prefer an English product name, else a barcode placeholder and manual naming. FoodSwitch, USDA Branded barcode lookups, FSANZ branded foods, and GS1 are **not integrated or authorised substitute providers**. Never automatically add external food records to the shared library.

External/reference/barcode items open the **same editable Food Item review**, with clear source attribution and an explicit **Add to library** action. Offer optional **Scan nutrition panel** photo capture/selection; on-device OCR must locate nutrient rows and the actual per-100 g/mL column, ask for manual column identification if uncertain, and never blindly treat the last OCR column or a serving column as per-100. Highlight extracted figures; ambiguous or missing figures require user review. Label photographs are not uploaded or retained; initial OCR model loading can require connectivity.

Food Item nutrients normally use a **per-100 g or per-100 mL** base. Display rows in NZ label order **energy (kJ and kcal adjacent), protein, fat, carbohydrate, fibre**, maximum two decimals in saved/displayed values. Keep small authentic label differences; for major (>2%) source energy discrepancy prefer source kJ as the conversion reference unless manually corrected. Editing kJ or kcal immediately converts the other at **1 kcal = 4.184 kJ**. Required energy, protein, fat, carbohydrate, and fibre must have values or **identified estimates**, never unlabelled zero for unknown. A small **Provide estimate** button alongside Fibre searches similar entries in the bundled databases, fills only sufficiently supported estimates, and displays/stores their food and source reference; if uncertain, leave it blank and explain. Editing the name, unit, or estimated value must invalidate a stale estimate reference. Brand, barcode, nutrient provenance, and units remain editable.

**Familiar measures:** Each is a named unit with an explicit g or mL per unit consistent with the food's base unit (e.g., one slice = 35 g), not a new food record. Record the selected quantity and unit as entered, converting nutrients through the defined measure. Choosing a whole/familiar unit initially sets quantity to **1**, not 100; choosing base g/mL defaults to 100. Keep stored legacy measure labels compatible. Remembering previously used food quantities is a possible later shortcut, not an implemented requirement.

**Water from foods:** The optional **Counts towards water intake** control sits below Scan nutrition panel, defaults off except Water (100% for Water without an explicit setting), and chooses 0–100% of the residual per-100 mass after protein, fat, carbohydrate, and fibre. Avoid double-subtracting fibre if carbohydrates already include it (USDA). Clamp residual at zero and use approximate 1 g/mL density for liquids where unknown. Only explicitly flagged foods contribute; nested recipes contribute their ingredient water proportionately and exactly once. Historical food entries retain a nutrient **and water snapshot**, so later library changes don't rewrite the diary retrospectively.

**Recipes:** Dishes can contain Food Items or Dishes; Meals can contain Food Items, Dishes, or Meals, with **no circular dependency**. Reuse the same library search and Food Item editor when adding ingredients; ingredient quantities use g, mL, valid familiar measures, or whole nested dishes/meals. Show ingredients **above** Add to Dish/Meal, with touch-friendly drag handles; save their explicit order, including linked variations. Derived recipe size, per-100 and full-recipe nutrients/water are calculated from its components; measured liquid mass may be approximate. Prevent deletion of a referenced ingredient. Linked recipes use stable IDs so source updates propagate according to the saved variation rules; do not impose automatic substitutions.

**Save and record:** Every FDM editor has a prominent solid **Record serving of [name]** button at the top, recording the current edited draft to the owner-private diary without changing the shared definition. When Save is pressed **after changing an existing FDM**, show a real popup, not a form extension, with exactly: **Record serving with these values → Update original [FDM type] → Save as new [FDM type]**. Closing or Escape keeps the edited draft; no Keep editing button. Save as new prompts for a unique name **within the same popup**, prefilled with the original name and cursor at its end; no separate variation form and no silently overwritten original. Food variations are distinct Food Items. Dish/Meal variations are linked to the edited parent by default, inherit later parent changes except explicit overrides, and can be detached. The link control reads **Update details when [parent name] is updated**. Nested variations are supported without circular links. New diary entries use saved template quantities, not the last instance's edits.

## 5. Activity Library and step reconciliation

The **Activity Library is one shared library**, just like FDM definitions; both accounts can create/edit saved named **instances** (e.g., a 7.66 km walk and a 10.5 km walk are separate entries), but their favourites and recorded activities are private. There are no Activity equivalents of Dishes/Meals. Reuse the FDM editor's field, button, search/favourites, Save popup, variation naming, and general layout conventions. A saved activity can define an activity type and relevant **time, distance, step count, or fallback subjective effort**. The saved details are defaults only: recording may adjust them, save a new named instance, or update the original. Date, calculated expenditure, and step-overlap decision belong to the **private recording**, not to the shared definition.

Calculate **one** activity expenditure estimate from the most informative measured inputs, never sum time-, distance-, and step-based estimates. For walking/running/hiking, distance + time yields speed/pace; manually entered steps are treated as accurate. Steps without distance yield an explicitly estimated distance from stride length; distance without steps can yield explicitly estimated steps. For supported cycling/swimming use distance/time to estimate intensity where appropriate. Distance-only or steps-only energy estimates are provisional. Subjective Light/Moderate/Vigorous effort is a fallback **only when objective inputs cannot determine intensity**, especially duration-only records. Require at least one useful measurement, but no separately entered calories burned.

**After pressing Record**, if an activity has entered or estimated steps, show a modal asking **“Are these steps additional to those recorded by Health Connect?”** (label estimated steps accordingly). The last Yes/No answer for that member **on that device** is preselected next time; an edited recording uses its own saved answer. The user can change it before recording. Never show this as an editor field or save the answer to the shared library. **Yes** adds these steps to the displayed daily total **alongside**, not inside, Health Connect's unchanged measured daily total. **No** means they are already included. Distinguish explicitly entered measured steps from distance/time-derived **estimated** steps, including an approximation marker when estimates contribute to the displayed total. Do not guess overlap, assume an unknown Health Connect day means zero measured steps, or add activity calories twice. Private activity entries remain editable/deletable and can be backdated. Preserve readability of old activity records.

## 6. Personal details and targets

Settings → **Details & Targets** contains optional **date of birth, height (cm), and sex for the energy equation**, then optional **Weight (kg), Water (litres), Steps, Protein (g), and Fibre (g)** goals. **Goal intensity** sits immediately below Weight and is Gentle, Moderate (default), or Strong, controlling the intended pace of weight change, **not** the width of the calorie range. Do not introduce an independent calorie target, range-width option, usual-activity-level selector, or fat/carbohydrate goals. Personal details and goals remain genuinely unset when blank; never import fictional sample goals.

Store details and goals together in owner-private `users/{uid}/settings/targets`, synchronised only between that member's devices. Do not enable editing until the stored settings have loaded, to avoid overwriting pending/offline data with blanks. **Save** returns to Settings; **Back** discards edits. The calculator may show a range only when date of birth, height, sex-for-equation, target weight, and an applicable genuine current weight are available (or when valid historical sample inputs are explicitly permitted under section 7). The Weight percent readout keeps the **actual ÷ target** convention.

## 7. Calorie budget and energy model

Provide a provisional **daily calorie-intake target range**, not a deadline or precise expenditure measurement, based on the current weight goal. Show this in the Calories readout and, on eligible completed days, as the original translucent range with exact dashed low/high boundaries. No manual calorie-budget override or assumed fat/carbohydrate targets.

`calorie-budget.js` implements **2023 National Academies adult (19+) EER equations** using the published inactive/low-active sex-specific coefficients, actual age, height, and applicable weight. Use the **most recent owner-private weigh-in on or before each personal date**, never a future weight. Before the first private weigh-in, genuine historical **sample weigh-ins and available sample steps** can drive calculations only on historical sample dates. Never use sample values to fill missing personal/current weight or steps. Signed-out sample mode displays sample intake without a personalised calorie-budget line. The sample CSV's old `budget`, `budgetLow`, and `budgetHigh` values are not used or present in the current bundle. No missing weigh-ins are interpolated.

Goal-intensity adjustments relative to estimated maintenance are: **loss** Gentle 5–10%, Moderate 10–15%, Strong 15–20% below; **gain** Gentle 2–5%, Moderate 5–8%, Strong 8–11% above; **at goal** ±2.5%. Moderate is the default. Range *width* arises independently from plausible activity variation. Determine whether at goal using the median of at least **three applicable weigh-ins in 14 days** within **0.25 kg**, otherwise the latest; use the latest for EER itself. No target date or linear kcal/kg weight-loss prediction.

EER already includes resting expenditure, routine activity, and digestion: never add resting metabolism or thermic effect again. Add only extra step energy above a **3,000 steps/day** heuristic baseline, approximately **0.0005 kcal/kg/metre** using estimated stride **0.415 × height(m)**; these are approximations, not exact individual costs. Use up to **14 completed step days in the previous 21 days**, requiring at least **three** for a reliable 20th/50th/80th-percentile distribution. Today's incomplete steps contribute observed progress; distribution predicts **only unobserved remainder**, not a fictitious activity. Completed historical days use only the data available for those dates. If private steps are completely absent, offer a conservative inactive-to-low-active maintenance range, not invented counts. Prior-day exercise is never projected into today. Bounds reflect maintenance uncertainty and goal-intensity edges and are rounded **outwards to the nearest 25 kcal**.

Recorded activities add **net** expenditure above rest (about `(MET − 1) × kg × minutes / 60`) with their own day-specific MET estimate. For step-overlapping activities, subtract step energy already counted in the reconciled step total before adding the single activity estimate. The calorie engine accepts date-specific `stepDays` and `activities` inputs from private Firestore listeners; source `dailyTotals` are never overwritten by activity totals. No routine double-counting, false assumption of a complete food diary, or invented exercise. The model remains provisional and uncalibrated against personal trends.

**Safety bounds:** Automatic budgets cover adults **19–110** with sufficient personal details; pregnancy/breastfeeding and other circumstances needing tailored advice are not yet screened. Suppress loss targets when current or target BMI is below **18.5**, do not automatically offer underweight maintenance, and suppress the **entire** calorie range if its low bound is below **1,200 kcal/day**, requesting review rather than silently flooring it. These guards do not establish individual safety or adequacy. Future calibration must use sustained evidence and validated dynamic models, not fixed kcal/kg assumptions.

**Model references:** [NASEM adult EER coefficients](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/food-nutrition/healthy-eating/dietary-reference-intakes/tables/equations-estimate-energy-requirement.html); [2024 Adult Compendium](https://pacompendium.com/adult-compendium/); [NASEM activity limits](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK591020/); [ACSM walking equation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7896743/); [NICE guidance](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng246/chapter/Physical-activity-and-diet). [NIH Body Weight Planner](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/weight-management/body-weight-planner) is a future-model reference, not an implemented component.

## 8. Accounts, persistence, and delivery

Firebase is restricted by Firestore rules to **two manually provisioned member UIDs**, with no self-registration. Shared definitions live in `approvedFoods` and `approvedActivities`; each member's private documents are under `users/{uid}`, including `weightEntries`, `foodEntries`, legacy `waterEntries`, `exerciseEntries`, `dailyTotals`, and `settings/{targets,favorites}`. Personal favourites are synchronised across their devices; old shared favourite flags are migration-only and not edited. Maintain owner-only access to personal entries and settings. Google popup login on GitHub Pages shows explicit connected/cached/unauthorised state. Unconfigured/signed-out prototype editing stays separate from account data. Offline writes must queue and sync without loss, including in the face of reconnects. Do not remove compatibility readers for existing genuine records merely because their old writing UI was retired.

Use live listeners and persistent local caching. Home metric readings must **not go blank** while individual Firebase streams reload: keep a same-calendar-day, device-local, account-specific last complete **display** snapshot until all private streams and targets have loaded, then refresh it. Clear on sign-out; never show one account's readings for another. This display cache is **not a backup**.

**Data safety:** Provide manual export and **automatic, recoverable, off-device historical backups**, separate from live sync and caching, with minimal administration. **No backup architecture has yet been agreed or implemented.** Protect both members' personal data and include a demonstrably usable restore process; do not mistake the existing manual food-library JSON copy for comprehensive protection. Native Firestore managed backups and export require billing; investigate an affordable secure solution before production reliance.

**Code reuse:** Both libraries share favourite-first search ranking, save/variation popups, and Firestore delta-batching, retaining domain-specific validation. FDM selection uses one Food Library typeahead and scan implementation. Form controls, date handling, quoted-delimited file parsing, energy conversions, and numeric scrubbing should have single implementations wherever behaviour is identical. Use the activity module for walking stride estimation in calorie calculations; recorded-food correction reuses nutrition validation and preserves the original entry's creation timestamp/identity. Owner-private deletion uses one helper. No unneeded second UI, unused import code, or version diary. The main app remains GitHub Pages; distribute updates as timestamped, versioned **delta ZIPs containing only changed files**, retaining unchanged deployed files. No Firebase rules updates or migration unless specifically required, with clear instructions if they are.

## 9. Android Health Connect companion

**PieChartCompanion v0.2.000** is a separate signed native Android project built by GitHub Actions. Each member uses their own authorised Google identity in the same Firebase project and grants Health Connect step access, including optional background permission. Supported first path is Android 14+ with Health Connect and steps data from the phone; no Huawei Health requirement or independent native sensor fallback yet. The companion aggregates the most recent **21 completed days plus today**, never uploads raw step events, and must not write zero when data/permission is absent.

Owner-private daily steps are merged into **`users/{uid}/dailyTotals/{YYYY-MM-DD}`** with fields `date`, `steps`, `complete`, `source:'health_connect'`, server `syncedAt`, and `timeZone`. Today is incomplete; earlier local days are complete. Store **one aggregate per date per user**, never sum multiple devices. The web listener accepts only valid Health Connect totals and ignores other dailyTotals fields. The phone-local time zone sets dates; travel across time zones remains to be validated. The companion can sync manually or with background scheduling when Android allows; device permissions/battery management can delay runs. No automatic addition of activity-specific steps into these source documents.

**Observed status:** The Xiaomi 13 Pro companion is installed, signed in, and successfully sends steps to the main app. Background access, battery-unrestricted mode, and background autostart were enabled; the user now reports unattended syncing behaving satisfactorily and no concerning battery consumption. One approximately 8 km walk gave a count within around **10%** of the watch, which is encouraging but not accuracy validation. Second-phone/Redmi testing, long-term cadence, and travel/date boundaries remain unverified. Do not reopen those as immediate blockers without new evidence.

## 10. Outstanding validation and work

- **Data protection:** Agree, implement, and test automatic off-device backups **and a full restore**; existing synchronisation, browser caches, and the manual food JSON copy are not backups.
- **Live data integrity:** Test both members' access, personal favourites, shared FDM/Activity changes, multi-device and offline/reconnect behaviour, record correction/deletion, and date-boundary handling. Do not silently overwrite another user's edits.
- **Calculation accuracy:** Test step/activity overlap and historical budget calculations against known scenarios; exercise MET estimates and clinical safeguards remain provisional, not clinically validated.
- **UI/workflows:** Continue refining layouts, form navigation, shortcuts, date selection, and numeric gesture feel as real-world use reveals issues. Food-label OCR, barcode comparisons, and per-food quantity shortcuts still need realistic testing.
- **Android:** Second-phone setup and longer-term time-zone/accuracy checks when useful; initial sync and battery behaviour have been satisfactory.

Keep this list short and current. Do not turn it into a per-version history or add speculative features without agreement.
